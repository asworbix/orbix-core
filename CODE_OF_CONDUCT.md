# Code of Conduct

Orbix Core is a small project with a simple rule: be straight with each other and keep the work in front.

## What we expect

- Be direct and be kind. Critique the code, the design, or the argument; not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are two people optimising for different things.
- Keep discussions on the work: issues, pull requests, and discussions are for building Orbix Core.
- Respect people's time. Reproduce before you report, read before you ask, and say what you tried.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeatedly reopening decisions that have been made.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock contributions that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders from the project's GitHub spaces. Report a problem privately to the maintainers through the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); reports are handled discreetly.

This applies in every project space: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any channel that represents the project.
