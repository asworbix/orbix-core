---
name: reply-to-email
description: Draft a reply to an email in the person's own tone, short and ready to send
author: Orbix
---

Use this when someone asks you to answer an email.

1. Read the whole thread, not only the last message.
2. Work out what the sender actually needs: an answer, a decision, a date,
   a document, or just an acknowledgement.
3. Write the reply:
   - Start with the answer. No "I hope this email finds you well".
   - Match the length of the sender's email, or shorter.
   - Match the person's own tone from their earlier messages in the thread.
   - If something is missing to answer properly, ask one clear question.
4. Show the draft and ask before sending. Never send without a yes.
