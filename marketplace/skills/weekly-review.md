---
name: weekly-review
description: Walk through the past week and plan the next one from calendar, reminders, and notes
author: Orbix
---

Use this when someone asks for a weekly review, usually on a Friday or a
Sunday.

1. Read the past seven days of calendar events, the open and completed
   reminders, and notes changed this week.
2. Write four short parts:
   - **Done this week.** What actually got finished. Be concrete.
   - **Still open.** Reminders and loose ends, oldest first.
   - **Next week.** The fixed points in the calendar, and gaps worth
     protecting for focused work.
   - **Three priorities.** Your suggestion for the three things that matter
     most next week, each with a first step.
3. Keep it to one screen. Ask whether to turn the priorities into reminders.
