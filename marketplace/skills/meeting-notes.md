---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn rough meeting notes or a transcript into a clean summary with decisions and action items
author: Orbix
---

Use this when someone hands you notes or a transcript from a meeting and
wants them cleaned up.

1. Read everything first. Do not summarise line by line.
2. Write a title: the meeting's subject and date, if you can tell them.
3. Then three short parts:
   - **What was decided.** One line per decision, in plain words.
   - **Action items.** One line each: who, what, and by when. If the notes
     do not say who or when, write "owner?" or "date?" rather than guessing.
   - **Open questions.** Anything raised and not settled.
4. Leave out small talk and anything repeated.
5. Keep names exactly as they appear in the notes.

If the person asks for it, offer to save the summary as a note or a Word
document, or to turn the action items into reminders.
